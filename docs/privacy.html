Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

This policy explains how DualCam handles your recordings, local creator tools, purchases, and support requests. No DualCam account is required.

1. Recorded Media and Local Tools

DualCam captures photos and videos with the cameras and, when enabled, microphone. Captures are saved first in the app's local media library. Video editing and silence analysis run on your device; silence analysis is not a cloud transcription service. Teleprompter scripts, framing presets, and app preferences are stored locally.

We do not operate a service that uploads or hosts your recorded media, scripts, or presets. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. This does not mean that the entire app works without a network connection: purchases and other Apple services communicate with Apple.

2. Permissions and Your Choices

  • Camera: used to take photos and record video, including supported simultaneous front and rear capture.
  • Microphone: used for video audio. Audio can be muted with the app's recording controls.
  • Photos: used when you choose to save a recording to your system Photos library. The app does not scan your existing Photos library.

You can change permissions in iOS Settings. Denying a permission may prevent the corresponding recording or saving function.

3. Apple Purchases and Entitlement Verification

DualCam Pro uses Apple's StoreKit and App Store services to load local prices, process purchases, restore purchases, and verify access. Apple handles your Apple Account, payment information, billing, and transaction history under Apple's privacy policy. DualCam does not receive your full payment-card details or Apple Account password.

The app receives StoreKit product and transaction information, such as product identifiers, transaction identifiers, subscription expiration, and refund or revocation status. It uses verified purchase information on the device to determine Pro access and respond to purchase updates. We do not send your recordings or local creator content to Apple as part of this entitlement check, and we do not operate our own transaction-verification server.

Choosing Restore Purchases asks Apple's App Store to synchronize purchase information and may require authentication. Apple maintains its transaction records separately from app storage; deleting DualCam does not cancel a subscription or delete Apple's billing records.

4. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

App recordings and creator content remain in local app storage until you remove them or delete the app, subject to your device's storage and backup settings. Deleting a recording within DualCam does not delete copies you previously exported to Photos, shared elsewhere, or retained in a system backup. Copies and backups are governed by the settings and policies of the services you use.

Back up recordings you want to keep. Save them to Photos or another destination before deleting the app. We cannot recover recordings from your device. Your existing media library remains accessible if Pro expires.

5. Sharing, External Links, and Support

When you choose Share or Save to Photos, iOS and your chosen destination handle that action. Their policies and your settings apply to exported copies. Opening App Store, review, subscription-management, or website links contacts the relevant service; those services may receive connection information under their own policies.

This legal and support website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which is subject to GitHub's privacy statement. If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to provide and use it to answer your request. Please do not send sensitive recordings or payment-card information. You may ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

6. Children and Privacy Requests

DualCam is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to support may contact us. You can delete local content, revoke device permissions, or contact us about support correspondence. Purchase-data requests are handled by Apple for the records it controls.
