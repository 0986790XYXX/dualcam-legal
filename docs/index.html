DualCam

Capture photos and video with compatible front and rear cameras together. Recordings are saved in the app first; choose Save to Photos or Share when you want to export.

Privacy Policy

Local recordings and creator tools, device permissions, Apple StoreKit purchase verification, sharing, and support privacy.

Terms of Service

Free and Pro features, weekly/monthly/yearly subscriptions, lifetime purchase, restoration, cancellation, and your recordings.

Support and Purchase Help

  • Find recordings: open the app's media library. Captures are not automatically added to Photos; open a recording and choose Save to Photos or Share.
  • Pro access: open DualCam Pro from Settings. Restore Purchases uses the Apple Account that made the purchase and may require a network connection or authentication.
  • Manage subscriptions: use the in-app subscription-management link or iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. A lifetime purchase does not cancel an existing subscription.
  • Keep your media: export important recordings before deleting the app. Pro expiration does not block access to recordings already in the app library.
  • Compatibility: simultaneous front/rear capture requires a supported iPhone. Available cameras and quality vary with hardware, temperature, and system conditions.

For help, email dosmallnw@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or private recordings.
